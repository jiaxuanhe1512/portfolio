works by Jiaxuan He info

Jiaxuan He is a graphic designer from Germany with a background in computer science, currently based in Karlsruhe.
His focus lies on print, web and photography. Currently he is pursuing his diploma at the Karlsruhe University of Arts and Design.

(c) 2026 Jiaxuan He

‘Leadership Special was written on a cup wrapped in newspaper.’, 2026, book (19.5 x 26 cm, 298 pp)
A visual diary with film photographs from years of travel and family visits across East and South-East Asia. Sorting and sequencing became an act of preservation, of photographs and memories alike, linked in non-linear ways. The book is printed on Munken Pure, thread-bound, with a UV-printed dust jacket on calligraphy paper.

‘Knight Playground’, 2025, typeface, specimen (A0), publication (14 x 20 cm, 16 pp)
Knight Playground is a display typeface developed using the description language Metafont, where letterforms are generated through code. The project explores rule-based design as a tool for creating typography, presented in a poster specimen and RISO publication.

‘Lammspieße und Waiguoren’, 2025, website
Lammspieße und Waiguoren is a website that compiles interactive, collage-style translations of everyday scenes in chinese-speaking environments along with recollections from my bicultural upbringing. The website was exhibited as part of “The Tired Immigrant” at Die Anstoß in Karlsruhe. Visit the website >>>here.

‘50 Cowboy hats with (A salesman known jovially as Cowboy...)’, 2026, publication (14 x 20 cm, 64 pp)
The publication is about 50 cowboy hats found on eBay, each evoking a fantasy and allure. It blurs the line between documentation and fantasy as the index remains ambiguous. The publication consists of an A2 poster as cover and the leather thread-bound main body.

‘Where One Sees’, 2025, psoter/publication (70 x 100 cm)
Where One Sees is a seminar by Alex Balgiu and Sophie Demay focusing on concrete poetry, specifically on Lenora de Barros’ works. Lu Kellert and I incorporated the playful, analog methods of our group projects into the design of the poster/publication. The posters were offset-printed.

Jiaxuan He (2000) is a German graphic designer, who was born in Beijing (CN). Before he began his studies as a graphic designer, he studied Computer Science at the University of Stuttgart, completing his Bachelor's degree in 2022. Shortly after, he began his studies in Graphic Design at the Karlsruhe University of Arts and Design. He works mainly in print, especially book and poster design, as well as web design and photography. Moreover, he is highly interested in typography, film and videography.
